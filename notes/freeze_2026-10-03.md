# Analysis freeze, 2026-10-03

Every number, table and figure in the paper comes from the files below, produced by the code below. Checksums: freeze/manifest_2026-10-03.sha256 (code, reference tables, outputs) and freeze/inputs_2026-10-03.sha256 (ADS-B caches and per-airport extracts). After this point, code or data change only to fix a verified bug, logged in notes/decisions.md with its effect, and the manifest is regenerated.

## What is frozen

* Detector: ops_detector.py, v3.1 (rule 12, parallel runways). v3.0.1 kept at notes/ops_detector_v3.0.1_2026-10-02.py. New England is identical under both.
* Measures: q2_measures.py with calibrated airspeed (--airspeed cas, default) and profile exponent 1/7 (default).
* Q2 sample: inferred approaches not scored (us_q2.py sets their status to "inferred"). Q1b keeps them.
* Weather: one-minute wind quality filter (wind_qc.py).
