# POH lookup report: BE36, BE58, PA32, P32R (agent report, 2026-10-02)

## Summary
I opened POH/AFM pages for every type except the G58 and the non-turbo PA-32-301. For those two, some values come only from a training manual or an accident report that quotes the POH, and the rest are "not found". Unless noted otherwise, each value is quoted from the PDF text layer and many were also checked visually against the rendered page. Every demonstrated-crosswind value below is a demonstrated figure, not a limit. The 2002 A36 POH says "The value shown is not limiting", and the Saratoga II HP POH (Fig 5-5) says "(Not a limitation)".

"pN" is the PDF page and "pgN" is the manualslib viewer page. Every manualslib URL below is `https://www.manualslib.com/manual/<id>/<name>.html?page=N`.

Year differences:
- **A36:** 17 kt on every A36 POH I opened. E-1 thru E-926 gives a 76 kt approach and a 56 KIAS white arc (3600 lb). E-2111 and later, and the G36, give 79 kt with flaps 30° and 61 KIAS (3650 lb).
- **Cherokee Six:** the 1974–76 manual is in MPH and CAS (20 mph demonstrated crosswind). The 1977+ POH is in knots (17 kt).

## Rows to double-check against a physical POH
1. **G58:** I found no crosswind or approach value in any opened document, and the 74 KIAS VSO is only from the FlightSafety training manual.
2. **PA-32-301 (non-turbo) Saratoga:** I opened no POH. The values come from the AAIB quote, and the approach speed was not found.
3. **A36 E-927 thru E-2110 (about 1976–83):** the POH exists (ee45728f75 CDN), but the download was refused (403). This range is a gap.
4. **A36 2002 POH:** the title page is missing, so the serial applicability is inferred from serial notes inside the manual.
5. **Saratoga SP (VB-1080):** there is no title page, so the serial range is unconfirmed.
6. **Cherokee Six 761-559:** values are in mph/CAS only, and "about 90 mph" is vague and doesn't say IAS or CAS.
7. **Cherokee Six VB-830:** the 47 KIAS white arc is low compared with the other PA-32s, though the Fig 5-3 chart agrees.
8. **Baron 58 TH-1389 / TH-1396 and after (5500 lb, P/N 58-590000-35 and later):** not opened. The approach speed and white arc may differ from the TH-773 POH.
9. **Not opened at all:** the 1976 Lance (VB-750) and the 1980–84 Turbo Saratoga SP (PA-32R-301T).

The PA-32-301T is fixed gear, so I've grouped it under the PA32 designator rather than P32R.
